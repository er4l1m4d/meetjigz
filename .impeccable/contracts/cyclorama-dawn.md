# Direction Contract: Cyclorama Dawn

## THESIS

A portfolio that treats each section as a lighting cue — the visitor scrolls through atmospheric phases from night to day, where light alone creates an infinite horizon behind the work.

## OWN-WORLD

Depthless cyc black as ground, a low cobalt horizon band as structure, rose gathering above it, white-day wash reserved for the fully active state. Lighting-plot stencil caps for cue names with tabular numerals. Content held in horizontal horizon bands stacked from dark to light.

## STORY

## FIRST VIEWPORT

Full-bleed gradient from depthless black at top through cobalt band at 30% to rose gathering at 60%. Developer name in white stencil caps, centered. A single "begin" CTA in the cobalt band. The gradient is the hero — no hero image needed.

## FORM

Theater lighting cyclorama. Horizon bands as sections. Atmospheric transitions between states. Cue-based navigation.
