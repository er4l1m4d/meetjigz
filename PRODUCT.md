# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary: Potential employers and clients evaluating the developer for jobs, freelance gigs, or collaborations. They scan quickly, looking for evidence of skill, taste, and relevant experience.

## Product Purpose

A personal developer portfolio that proves competence through demonstrated work, establishes trust through clear positioning, and converts interest into contact or opportunity.

## Positioning

A developer portfolio that shows work through detailed case studies rather than just thumbnails — proving process and reasoning, not just final results.

## Operating Context

Visitors arrive from job applications, LinkedIn, GitHub, or referrals. They have 30-60 seconds to decide if this developer is worth deeper investigation. The portfolio must communicate skill level, tech stack, and personality within that window.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Password-gated admin console for content management (CRUD)
- Hero section with name, role, tagline, CTAs
- About section with bio and current build status
- Skills section with categorized technologies
- Featured works grid with project cards linking to case studies
- Contact section with social links
- Case study pages with evidence galleries
- Admin console for content management (CRUD)
- Dark/light theme toggle
- Responsive design

## Product Principles

1. Show, don't tell — demonstrate competence through work, not claims
2. Respect the visitor's time — communicate value within seconds
3. Make contact frictionless — clear CTAs, easy social links
4. Maintain technical credibility — clean code, modern stack, thoughtful architecture
5. Balance personality with professionalism — distinctive without being distracting
